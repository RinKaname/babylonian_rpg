# The Tamkarum Guild: Ancient Trade Corridors & Foreign Arbitrage Guide

In ancient Mesopotamia, wealth was not solely forged in the alluvial mud of the Euphrates. The richest patricians of Babylon—such as the historical merchant banking dynasty of **Iddin-Marduk**—built their empires by financing long-distance merchant expeditions (**Tamkārū**) to foreign realms.

Under the **Code of Hammurabi (§§ 100–107)**, merchant investors entrust silver capital, transport fleets, and export goods to an accredited Tamkarum. The merchant journeys across deserts, mountain passes, and oceanic gulfs to sell Babylonian manufactures at premium foreign markups and return laden with raw minerals and pure silver coin.

---

## The Core Merchant Arbitrage Loop

1. **Escape Domestic Gluts**: When your workshops or farms flood the local Kārum Quay, local prices crash to `Abundant` (hitting the 25% floor). Foreign kings don't have your local oversupply; they pay **$1.3\times$ to $2.8\times$ base price** for Babylonian goods!
2. **Import Scarce Strategic Materials at Source Wholesale**: Mesopotamia has no native tin, copper, or hardwood timber. Caravans allow you to buy these materials directly from foreign mines and quarries at up to **50% below Babylon market prices**, delivering them straight to your workshop sacks.
3. **Repatriate Pure Silver Coin**: If you don't need raw materials, instruct your Tamkarum to select **`[S] Pure Silver Coin`** to convert 100% of foreign sales revenue into physical silver shekels!

## 4 Proven Merchant Arbitrage Playbooks

### Playbook 1: The Chariot & Arms Exporter (The 180 Silver Play)
1. Use Corvée gangs (`[8]->[1]`) to gather free timber logs from the Euphrates river commons.
2. Forge Bronze Tools and assemble **Spoked War Chariots** in your workshop (`[5]->[12]`).
3. Load the Chariots into an Anatolian or Meluhha expedition (`[4]`).
4. Select directive **`[S] Pure Silver Coin`** or **`[1] Prioritize Tin`**.
5. **Outcome**: Pocket **180.0 to 192.0 silver per chariot** (instead of the 30 silver Babylon glut price) and bring back mountains of Cassiterite Tin!

### Playbook 2: The Magan Copper Pipeline
1. Sow an agricultural estate with barley; harvest with 4 oxen for thousands of qa grain.
2. Charter a Gulf Seagoing Vessel to **Magan** (7 days, 105 qa fodder).
3. Pack surplus barley and beer from your granary (sells at $+80\%$ foreign markup).
4. Set directive to **`[1] Prioritize copper_ore`**.
5. **Outcome**: The Tamkarum buys raw copper at **2.20 silver/ea** (vs. 4.00 in Babylon) and unloads it directly into your foundry storehouse!

### Playbook 3: The Textile Merchant of Meluhha
1. Keep a flock of sheep or buy raw wool on the market; weave bolts of **Standard Woolen Garment** in your manufactory (`[5]->[5]`).
2. Charter an expedition to **Meluhha** (28 days).
3. Wool cloth sells in Meluhha for an astonishing **8.40 silver per bolt** ($2.8\times$ base!).
4. Bring back luxury cylinder seals (8 silver) and rare perfumes (6 silver) to sell in Babylon for **15–20 silver each**!

### Playbook 4: Pure Cash Arbitrage
1. Send an expedition carrying only an entrusted silver purse (e.g. 50–100 silver) without export goods.
2. Select target import for a single scarce material in Babylon (e.g. `tin`).
3. The Tamkarum buys wholesale abroad, lands at Babylon's quay, deposits the goods into your sacks, and automatically expands Babylon's domestic supply for immediate profit!
